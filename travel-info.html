Baggage

What you can bring

Every traveler gets a checked allowance and a carry-on limit, based on the cabin you book.

CabinChecked baggageCarry-on
Economy20 kg7 kg
Comfort25 kg10 kg
Business30 kg12 kg

The checked allowance is per traveler, so a booking for two in Comfort includes 50 kg in total. Need more? Add extra checked baggage in 5 kg steps at PHP 300 per kg when you search, and it appears on your fare total straight away.

Carry-on

One bag plus one personal item per traveler. Keep valuables, medication, documents, and anything needed in flight in the cabin, not in the hold.

Prohibited items

No flammables, compressed gas, corrosive liquids, or unapproved lithium batteries in either bag. Lighters and power banks must travel in the cabin.

Special items

Sports gear, musical instruments, and mobility aids are accepted, but may need to be declared up to 48 hours before departure.

Damaged or lost bags

Report it before leaving the airport so we can open a file. Keep your baggage tag until the bag is confirmed delivered.
